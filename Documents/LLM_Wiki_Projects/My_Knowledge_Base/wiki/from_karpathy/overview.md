# Overview

This wiki covers two major technology domains: **programming languages and systems software** (centered on Rust), and **artificial intelligence** (spanning machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI).

## Domain Map

### Programming Languages & Systems
- **[Rust](entities/rust-programming-language.md)** is a multi-paradigm systems language that achieves memory safety without a garbage collector.
- Its core innovations—[ownership](concepts/ownership-system.md), [borrowing](concepts/borrow-checker.md), and [type safety](concepts/type-safety.md)—are increasingly influential across the industry.
- Rust emerged from [Mozilla](entities/mozilla.md), was created by [Graydon Hoare](entities/graydon-hoare.md), and is now stewarded by the [Rust Foundation](entities/rust-foundation.md).
- Adoption spans browsers (Firefox/Servo), cloud infrastructure (AWS), operating systems (Linux kernel), and embedded devices.

### Artificial Intelligence
- **[AI](sources/artificial-intelligence.md)** research pursues reasoning, knowledge representation, planning, learning, natural language, and perception.
- **[Machine learning](concepts/machine-learning.md)** is the dominant approach, with **[deep learning](concepts/deep-learning.md)** and **[neural networks](concepts/neural-networks.md)** driving the current boom.
- **[Natural language processing](concepts/natural-language-processing.md)** and **[computer vision](concepts/computer-vision.md)** are the most mature application areas.
- **[Generative AI](concepts/generative-ai.md)**—powered by large language models and diffusion models—has transformed text, image, and code generation since 2020.
- Key industry players include [OpenAI](entities/openai.md), [Google DeepMind](entities/google-deepmind.md), and [IBM](entities/ibm.md).

### Connections
- Both domains intersect in systems infrastructure for AI (e.g., Rust-based ML frameworks, GPU drivers) and in AI-assisted software development tools.
- Safety and reliability are shared concerns: Rust's compile-time guarantees and AI's alignment and robustness challenges both seek to prevent harmful failures.
