# OpenAI

## Summary

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research and deployment company known for developing the GPT series of large language models and ChatGPT. It has played a central role in the generative AI boom of the 2020s.

## Key Points

- Created ChatGPT, a widely used conversational AI based on GPT (generative pre-trained transformer) architecture.
- GPT models are pre-trained on large text corpora to predict the next token, then fine-tuned with reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).
- Current models are prone to hallucinations (generating false information), which RLHF aims to reduce.
- Competes with Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), Copilot (Microsoft), and Meta AI.
