# IBM

## Summary

IBM is a multinational technology corporation with a long history in artificial intelligence research. It developed Deep Blue, the first computer chess system to defeat a reigning world champion, and Watson, a question-answering system that won on Jeopardy!.

## Key Points

- Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov on May 11, 1997.
- Watson defeated Jeopardy! champions Brad Rutter and Ken Jennings in 2011.
- IBM's early AI work demonstrated the potential of specialized hardware and search algorithms for narrow, high-profile tasks.
