# Rust Foundation

## Summary

The Rust Foundation is an independent non-profit organization formed in February 2021 to steward the Rust programming language and its ecosystem. It was created in response to Mozilla layoffs to ensure Rust's long-term sustainability.

## Key Points

- Announced on February 8, 2021, by five founding companies: AWS, Google, Huawei, Microsoft, and Mozilla.
- Takes ownership of Rust trademarks and domain names and provides financial support through grants and server funding.
- Faced community pushback in 2023 over a draft trademark policy; the foundation subsequently apologized and revised the policy.
- The Moderation Team resigned in November 2021 in protest over governance accountability concerns.
