# Google DeepMind

## Summary

Google DeepMind is a leading AI research laboratory formed from the merger of DeepMind and Google Brain. It is known for breakthroughs in game-playing AI, protein folding, and mathematical reasoning.

## Key Points

- Developed AlphaGo, the first computer Go-playing system to defeat a professional Go player (Lee Sedol, 2016).
- Created AlphaFold 2, which approximates 3D protein structures in hours rather than months.
- Developed mathematical reasoning models including AlphaTensor, AlphaGeometry, AlphaProof, and AlphaEvolve.
- Experimental Gemini Deep Think model achieved gold medal results in the International Math Olympiad 2025.
