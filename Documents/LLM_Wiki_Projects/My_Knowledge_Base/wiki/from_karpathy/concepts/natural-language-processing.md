# Natural Language Processing

## Summary

Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of AI focused on enabling computers to read, write, and communicate in human languages. It encompasses speech recognition, machine translation, information extraction, and question answering.

## Key Points

- Early NLP was based on generative grammar and semantic networks, struggling with word-sense disambiguation outside small "micro-worlds."
- Modern NLP relies on word embeddings, transformers, and attention mechanisms.
- GPT (generative pre-trained transformer) models, starting in 2019, generate coherent text by predicting the next token.
- By 2023, GPT-class models achieved human-level scores on the bar exam, SAT, and GRE.
- Current models include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta AI.
- Multimodal models can process text, images, video, and audio together.
